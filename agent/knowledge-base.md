TABLE MOUNTAIN AERIAL CABLEWAY – KNOWLEDGE BASE (rates valid 1 Jul 2026 – 30 Jun 2027)

ABOUT
The Cableway takes visitors from the Lower Station on Tafelberg Road to the top of Table Mountain (1,060 m above sea level) in a few minutes, in rotating cable cars with 360-degree views. At the top: walking trails, viewpoints, the Ten67 Eatery and the VIEWS wine bar.

CONTACT
Phone: 021 424 8181 | Email: info@tablemountain.net | Refunds: refunds@tablemountain.net
Live status, weather and queue times: tablemountain.net or the Table Mountain Aerial Cableway app (App Store / Google Play)

OPERATING HOURS
Hours change with the season and the weather. Current posted hours: first car up 07:30, last car up 19:00, last car down 20:00. Always check the live status on the day.
The Cableway runs weather permitting and may close in bad weather (wind, cloud, rain).

STANDARD TICKETS (return / one way)
Online Adult (18+): R475 / R300
Online Child (4–17): R230 / R165
Ticket Office Adult: R530 / R330
Ticket Office Child (4–17): R270 / R180
Children under 4: free
Buying online is cheaper than buying at the ticket office.

CONCESSIONS AND SPECIALS
SA Senior (South African citizens 60+, valid SA ID): R130 return / R70 one way. Online and ticket office, 7 days a week.
Student (valid local or international student card): R320 return / R200 one way. Ticket office at the Lower Station ONLY.
Local is Lekker (South Africans, SA ID required to buy and on the day): Adult R265 return, Child R135 return. Valid 1–30 November 2026 only. Online only, must be bought BEFORE the day of the visit (no same-day sales).
Birthday Special: a FREE ticket for South Africans during their birthday month, with a valid SA ID, driver's licence or birth certificate. Redeem at the ticket office or Visitor Information Centre (not online).
Birthday Fast Track upgrade: available at the ticket office for SA citizens with a 13-digit ID; ask the ticket office for the price.

FAST TRACK (skip the queue, all ages, standard price – no concessions)
Return: R1,200 – online or Lower Station ticket office
One way: R750 – ticket office only (Lower Station going up, Top Station going down)
Daily numbers are limited, so book online in advance. Look for the yellow Fast Track entrance near vida e caffè at the Lower Station. A standard ticket can be upgraded at the ticket office or in Webtickets under "My Tickets" > "Upgrade".

VIRTUAL CABLE CARD (for regular visitors, valid 1 Jul 2026 – 30 Jun 2027)
Adult R1,700 | Child (4–17) R850 | Pensioner 60+ (SA ID) R950 | Fast Track R6,000

HOW TO BUY
Online (Webtickets): https://www.webtickets.co.za/v2/event.aspx?itemid=681080
WhatsApp: choose "Buy via WhatsApp" on the Webtickets page (return and one-way tickets only, no specials).
Ticket office at the Lower Station (higher prices; needed for Student, Birthday Special and one-way Fast Track).
Only tickets from Webtickets, Webtickets WhatsApp or City Sightseeing South Africa are valid. Tickets from other websites are NOT accepted.
Online tickets can be printed or shown on a phone. You may still queue on busy days.

VALIDITY, WEATHER AND REFUNDS
Online, ticket office and WhatsApp tickets are valid for 7 days.
If you miss your trip because of weather or time, use your online ticket on the next open day within that week, or claim a refund online. WhatsApp tickets are fully refundable if unused within 7 days.
Fast Track: reschedule within 7 days or request a refund in Webtickets under "My Tickets". If the Cableway is closed on your day, you can reschedule or get a refund.
Refund questions: refunds@tablemountain.net

GETTING THERE
Address: Lower Cable Station, Tafelberg Road, Cape Town.
Park & Ride: park free at the Lower Tafelberg Road parking area and take the FREE Cableway shuttle to the Lower Station. Parking at the Lower Station is limited.
MyCiTi bus: routes 106 and 107 (Civic Centre – Camps Bay) stop at the top of Kloof Nek Road.
The City Sightseeing bus also stops at the Cableway.

ACCESSIBILITY
Wheelchair accessible: ramps and lifts at the Lower Station, paved paths at the top, accessible toilets at both stations, an elevator to the VIEWS wine bar, and wheelchair-friendly ramps at Ten67 Eatery. Mobility scooters are available at the Top Station (ask on arrival). Limited disabled parking near the Lower Station – show your disabled parking disc to staff.

NOT IN THIS KNOWLEDGE BASE – refer to 021 424 8181 / info@tablemountain.net
Today's weather or open status, exact queue times, group or tour-operator bookings, events and functions, pets, hiking route safety, Birthday Fast Track price, discounts not listed above.
